EMI Calculator – Loan ki Kist Kaise Calculate Hoti Hai?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) ka matlab hota hai loan ki ek fix monthly kist jo aap bank ya finance company ko har mahine dete hain. EMI mein principal aur interest dono shamil hote hain.

Interest Kaise Lagta Hai?

Bank interest yearly rate par charge karta hai, lekin EMI calculation monthly basis par hoti hai. Shuru ke mahino mein interest zyada hota hai aur dheere-dheere principal ka hissa badhta hai.

EMI Calculation Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1)
