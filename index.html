What is EMI?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed payment you make every month to repay a loan. It includes both principal and interest.

How EMI is calculated in India?

EMI depends on loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure. Banks in India follow standard EMI calculation formula.
